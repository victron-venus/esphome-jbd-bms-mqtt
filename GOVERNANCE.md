# Governance and responsibilities

esphome-jbd-bms-mqtt is maintained by the GitHub account [4alvit](https://github.com/4alvit). The maintainer decides scope and compatibility, reviews contributions, maintains repository/CI access, responds to security reports and owns release acceptance. Contributors propose changes through issues and pull requests; automated reviewers and CI provide evidence but do not replace accountable human decisions.

Discuss substantial behavior changes in an issue or pull request before implementation. Record the problem, alternatives, compatibility impact and validation in the review. The maintainer makes the final decision after considering that evidence. Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), [community conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [security reporting](SECURITY.md) and [release process](docs/release-workflow.md).

## Continuity limitation

There is currently **no designated backup maintainer and no established mechanism that restores repository, CI and release access within one week** if the primary maintainer is unavailable. A bot, public fork or automated workflow does not satisfy this requirement. OpenSSF Silver access continuity and a two-person bus factor remain unmet. The next governance improvement is to establish and verify a recovery arrangement with an authorized person; no unconfirmed person or recovery procedure is claimed here.
